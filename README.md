## 🎮 About

Kero's Batocera Flash 120 is a customized, preconfigured Batocera gaming image designed to make getting into retro gaming easier.

No complicated initial setup. No unnecessary subscriptions. Just a gaming environment ready to boot.

**100% Free. No paid upgrades.**

## ✨ Features

- Preconfigured Batocera environment.
- Curated gaming library.
- Controller-friendly interface.
- Multiple emulation platforms.
- Ready-to-use configurations.
- Space for additional games and user data.
- Completely free to use.

## 💾 Storage Information

| Specification | Details |
|---|---|

## ⚙️ Compatibility

Built around Batocera's emulation environment.

Performance depends on your hardware, graphics capabilities, and the individual emulator.

A compatible 64-bit system, suitable storage device, display, and controller are recommended.
